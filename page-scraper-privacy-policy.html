Privacy Policy for Page Scraper

Last updated: September 23, 2025

Overview

Page Scraper is a Chrome extension designed to extract and clean web page content for analysis, research, and data collection purposes. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how our extension handles your data.

Information We Collect

Data Processed Locally

Page Scraper processes the following information locally on your device:

  • Web page content: Text content from web pages you choose to scrape
  • Page metadata: Page titles, URLs, word counts, and quality metrics
  • Scraping history: Up to 20 recently scraped pages stored locally in your browser
  • Usage timestamps: When you scraped each page for organizing your history

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect any personal information about you
  • We do not track your browsing habits or website visits
  • We do not collect analytics or usage statistics
  • We do not access pages unless you explicitly activate the extension
  • We do not store any data on external servers

How We Use Your Information

All data processing occurs locally on your device. We use the collected information solely to:

  • Extract and clean content from web pages you choose to scrape
  • Display scraped content in an organized, readable format
  • Maintain a local history of your scraped pages for easy access
  • Generate quality metrics and statistics about the extracted content
  • Enable export functionality (copy, download, bulk download)

Data Storage

Local Storage Only

  • All scraped content and history is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API
  • No data is transmitted to external servers or third parties
  • Data persists locally until you clear it using the extension's "Clear History" feature or uninstall the extension

Data Retention

  • The extension automatically maintains up to 20 recent scraping sessions
  • Older entries are automatically removed when the limit is exceeded
  • You can manually clear all history at any time using the "Clear History" button

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. All processing occurs locally on your device, and no information is transmitted externally.

Permissions Explanation

Page Scraper requests the following permissions, used exclusively for core functionality:

  • scripting: To inject content extraction scripts into web pages you choose to scrape
  • activeTab: To access content on the currently active tab when you activate the extension
  • storage: To save your scraping history locally in your browser
  • host_permissions ("<all_urls>"): To enable scraping functionality on any website you visit

These permissions are used only when you explicitly activate the extension and only affect the current tab you're viewing.

Your Rights and Controls

You have full control over your data:

  • View your data: All scraped content is visible in the extension interface
  • Delete your data: Use the "Clear History" button to remove all stored content
  • Export your data: Download your scraped content as text files
  • Uninstall: Removing the extension deletes all associated data

Data Security

Since all data processing occurs locally on your device:

  • Your scraped content never leaves your computer
  • No network transmission of sensitive content occurs
  • Data security depends on your device's local security measures
  • No external authentication or accounts are required

Children's Privacy

Page Scraper does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The extension processes only the web content that users explicitly choose to scrape.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do:

  • The "Last updated" date at the top of this policy will be revised
  • If changes are significant, we will update the extension version
  • Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy

Open Source Transparency

Page Scraper is open source software. You can review the complete source code to verify our privacy practices and see exactly how your data is handled.

Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the extension's data practices, please:

  • Review the source code for complete transparency
  • Create an issue in the project repository
  • Contact us through the Chrome Web Store developer page

Compliance

This extension is designed to comply with:

  • Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) principles
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) requirements
  • Other applicable privacy regulations

Summary

In simple terms: Page Scraper works entirely on your device, stores data only locally in your browser, and never sends any information to external servers. You have complete control over your data and can delete it at any time.
